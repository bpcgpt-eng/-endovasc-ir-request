Important: The interactive buttons do not work inside the iPhone Files/WhatsApp document preview. This form must be opened from a hosted HTTPS website in Safari.

How to send the completed referral on iPhone: Fill the form, then select Send by WhatsApp. WhatsApp will open with the completed referral text. Choose the IR contact and send it. The first line contains the patient name as the referral title.

Alternative: Select Share referral file to share a completed text document named with the patient’s name through the iPhone share sheet.

Emergency rule: In septic shock, active major bleeding, severe haemodynamic instability, or rapid clinical deterioration, contact IR immediately while resuscitation and completion of available information continue.
